What changed since round 2

  • The light around a subject now fades out over about two inches, so it reads as warmth falling on the figure, not a cut-out. Sharper light and the line around the edge wait until outlines are traced on your tapestry.
  • Carry is gone; Fade and Drift remain.
  • Glints no longer follow the subjects. They appear one at a time, wherever a bright edge could plausibly catch light: wing feathers, folds of the robe, the white bird, leaves against the sky. They rise and fall over about a second, in the light's own colour.
  • "Along a thread" stretches each glint sideways along the weave and lets it slide a few threads, the way light runs along a fibre. "Pinpoint" is a single soft point.
  • "Show one now" makes a glint immediately, so you can judge one without waiting.

The subject outlines here are rough, traced by eye on the Met's photograph; in the app they will be traced on a photo of your tapestry, and the light can then follow the real edges. A phone cannot show a glint at true size: on the wall one is about a third of an inch long, here it is drawn a little larger so you can see it at all. An oil painting photographs sharper than wool, and projected light on fabric looks softer than a screen. Text is drawn as light added to the wall, so it fades as the room brightens.
