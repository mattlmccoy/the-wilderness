# Delights: seasons, Easter eggs and a richer wall (Matt, 2026-11-16: "Let's do them all.")

Rules that bind every item: light only (the projector can only add light: hats, stars, flames are
drawn as glowing lines and glows); no text off the tapestry (calm canvas zones only); TDD for every
piece of logic (core, Swift Testing); UI verified in the simulator; nothing pushed or installed
without Matt saying so. Calls marked (mine) are my design decisions, open to change.

## Core logic (WildernessCore, test-first)
- **Church calendar** `ChurchCalendar`: Easter by the Gregorian computus; Ash Wednesday, Palm
  Sunday, Pentecost, Advent Sunday (4th Sunday before Christmas), Christmas, Epiphany. Season of a
  date (advent, christmas, epiphany/ordinary, lent, holyWeek, easter, pentecost day, ordinary).
- **Counters**: Lent day n of 40 (Sundays not counted, Ash Wednesday = day 1); Advent candles lit
  (one per Advent Sunday passed, max 4).
- **Season effects** `Seasons.effects(on:)`: christmas (1 Dec–6 Jan: hats, snow), star (24–25 Dec
  and 6 Jan), easterDawn (Easter Sunday 5:00–11:00), halloween (31 Oct after 17:00).
- **Sun** `SunClock`: sunrise/sunset for a date and place (NOAA approximation); home = Atlanta
  (mine: no location permission on tvOS; settable later).
- **Day light** `DayLight.at(time, sun)`: warmth (colour temperature) and level: warm near
  sunrise/sunset, neutral midday, candle-warm and dimmer after dark, dimmest 23:00–05:00.
- **Liturgical tint** for the background wash: violet (Advent, Lent), white-gold (Christmas,
  Easter), red (Pentecost day, Palm Sunday), green (ordinary).
- **Moments** at rest: a few rare events an evening (stag leaps, raven's eye glints, angels
  brighten together), times seeded by the date so they are unpredictable but reproducible.
- **Secret code** `SecretCode`: ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → Select toggles the dreamcoat for 60 s.
- **Slow looking** `SlowLooking`: a seven-minute path through the figures, ~35 s each, Christ first
  and last; a bowl breath at each; one opening line.
- **Wind-down** `WindDown`: from a set time (default 22:30) the wall settles to breathing only; 30 min
  later the app lets the Apple TV sleep (idle timer back on), so no remote reach-out is needed.
- **Flashlight** protocol: `PhoneCommand.flashlight(Point?)`; nil ends it.

## On the wall (app)
1. Christmas: glowing hats on the lion, stag and bowing animal; snow (glints fall slowly instead
   of twinkling); the star over the angels. 2. Advent candles / Lent day line, small, on canvas.
3. Dreamcoat: every outline cycles the rainbow. 4. Easter dawn sweep. 5. Moments. 6. Halloween eyes
   (eye points marked by hand from the painting). 7. Flashlight pool following the phone.
8. Day light colour + level on all light. 9. Liturgical wash tint. 10. Slow looking (Menu → "Be
   still"). 11. Restoration reveal: on the restoration Context page the angels' outlines draw in,
   then glow. 12. Wind-down. 13. Siri: phone App Shortcuts ("Start the Wilderness demo", "Wilderness
   light only", "Flashlight") send the command to the wall.
